- :class:`conformal.ConformalRegressor` and
  :class:`conformal.ConformalClassifier` provide model-agnostic split
  conformal prediction: prediction intervals for regression and candidate
  label sets for classification with a finite-sample marginal coverage
  guarantee under exchangeability. They support one- and two-sided
  intervals, adaptive interval widths, LAC and randomized APS classification
  scores, sample weights, group-aware splits with per-group (Mondrian)
  coverage, cross-conformal fitting, and bitwise-stable pickle persistence.
  By :user:`scikit-learn contributors`.
